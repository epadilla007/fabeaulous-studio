# Narrative Map: Fabeaulous Feet

Visitor: nail tech, pedicurist, podology-adjacent professional, salon owner or distributor in Mexico, Canada or the US. One action: **request professional pricing** (WhatsApp or email), or apply to distribute.

## Homepage beats

| # | Visitor question | Section | Emotional shift | Single message | Proof type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who are you? | Hero | Curiosity to recognition | A professional pedicure system: file, steel tip, fresh discs for every client. | Visual (real technician at work) |
| 2 | Why should I care? | Manifesto (dark) | Recognition to relief | Blades cut clients and the same motion wears out your hands. This system fixes both. | Experiential (named injuries techs know) |
| 3 | How does it work? | Three steps (stacking cards) + flat tip explainer | Relief to understanding | Peel on, REMOVE, POLISH, peel off. No pressure. | Visual + mechanism |
| 4 | What do I get? | The system (horizontal gallery) | Understanding to desire | Every piece of the station, from file to extraction lamp. | Visual (product photography) |
| 5 | Who else trusts you? | Specs wall + pro quote + distributor countries | Desire to confidence | Real specs, a real salon owner, distributors in four countries. | Data + social |
| 6 | What do I do now? | Two paths + final CTA | Confidence to action | Ask for pricing for your salon, or carry the line. | Direct action |

## Transitions

- **1 to 2:** Hero image recedes (scale 1 to 1.08, dims) while the navy manifesto slides over it. Color crossfade from porcelain to navy.
- **2 to 3:** Sticky manifesto words finish lighting at 50% of the section, then the section releases into the porcelain steps section.
- **3 to 4:** Stacking cards settle; the disc motif circle draws its stroke as the gallery enters.
- **4 to 5:** Pinned horizontal gallery releases into the lilac specs wall; counters run.
- **5 to 6:** Quote sits in a void layout, then the two paths enter as a split, closing on the plum CTA band.
