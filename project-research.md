## Business facts

- **Name:** Fabeaulous Feet, Professional Pedicure System
- **Owners:** William Gordon Johnson (product development), Karina Martinez (business operations; also runs her own salon)
- **Base:** Nuevo Vallarta, Bahía de Banderas, Nayarit, Mexico. Orders ship from Mexico by courier (UPS, DHL).
- **Origin:** "We designed the Fabeaulous Feet Pedicure System in Canada" (live site)
- **Email:** info@fabeaulousfeet.com (sales@fabeaulousfeet.com also listed on the live site)
- **Distribution:** distributors listed in Canada, the United States, Portugal and Mexico
- **Trade shows:** ESI Show Toronto (Apr 2026), Vancouver expo, Guadalajara, Puebla
- **Warranty and policy:** Electric file 12-month limited warranty. 14-day replacement for manufacturing problems. Returns only for transit damage or wrong item. All sales final. The buyer pays import duties and taxes.

## Product inventory (real names only)

1. **Fabeaulous Feet Electric Pedicure File, Complete System**: 12V, 9 speeds, dual LED lights, 3.5 m (11 ft) cord and power adapter, Stainless Steel Tip, 60 discs (30 REMOVE, 30 POLISH). Colors: Purple, Silver, Caramel, Chocolate, Black. Drops to low speed if the tech presses too hard. Not cordless. Professional use only.
2. **Disposable Dermabrasion Discs**: REMOVE (coarse, blue, heavy callus, corns, dry and peeling skin), POLISH (fine, gray, whole foot). Packs: 300 REMOVE, 300 POLISH, 200 MIXED (100 + 100). 30-disc packs sold in Mexico. Self-adhesive, one per client, single use.
3. **Fabeaulous Feet Stainless Steel Tip**: medical grade stainless steel, flat rounded surface, 3.5 mm shaft, for the Fabeaulous file only. Includes 30 + 30 discs.
4. **2-Piece Stainless Steel Tip**: 1/8 in (3.18 mm) shaft for compatible rotary files. Includes 30 + 30 discs.
5. **LED MagniVac Vacuum Lamp**: quiet dust vacuum, washable easy-change filter (2 included), 30 LED lights, 3x and 8x magnifiers, multi-adjustable arm, mounting clamp. Stand version sold by distributors.
6. **Mini E-File with Tri-Ball Accessory**: portable, universal head, takes your own bits for acrylic or gel.
7. **Tri-Ball Accessory**: diamond-particle balls on 3/32 in (2.38 mm) shafts for cuticle reduction without nipping.
8. **Pedi-Cone Accessory**: diamond-particle cone, 3/32 in shaft, for dry skin between the toes.
9. **Power Adapter**: 12V DC, 4A, 3.5 m cord.
10. **Mexico salon line**: Mint Me foot cream (mint, urea, glycerin; 1 kg, 250 ml, 100 ml), Scrub Me exfoliating gel (medium grain, mint), pedicurist stool with 5-height footrest, client chair with 3 heights and a lamp mount.
11. **In development (per client call notes, Dec 2025):** a new disc design, patent pending, and a fan. No specs published.

## Competitor intel: 4blanc.com

- Sells dust collectors and lamps with a clinical, spec-heavy voice (HEPA grades, airflow, filter life).
- Strength: precise specs build trust with pros. Gap: little human story, no callus-reduction system.
- Fabeaulous difference: a complete callus-reduction method (tip + two-step discs + file + extraction lamp) designed by working pedicure professionals, with a bilingual MX/CA/US presence.
